## Short blurb (~100 words — Amazon description, app store, meta description)

Most people never read what the Buddha actually said. The texts are out there, but
they're written for scholars: 2,600-year-old Pali wrapped in a century of academic
English.

Plain Dharma is the six foundational teachings, told plainly. The first three talks he
gave. The instructions on loving-kindness. The foundations of mindfulness. And his answer
to how you decide what to believe. About an hour to read, start to finish.

It's free — the text, the audiobook, and the art are all Creative Commons Zero. Copy it,
print it, translate it, sell it. No permission needed, ever.

## Long blurb (~250 words — press, newsletter, Goodreads, long-form listings)

What the Buddha actually taught — before the schools, the systems, and the centuries of
commentary built on top of it.

Six teachings. He starts by ruling out both dead ends, chasing pleasure and punishing
yourself, and lays out the four noble truths for the first time. Days later he takes
apart the thing you call yourself. Then, to a thousand former fire-worshippers, he says
everything is burning — with wanting, anger, and confusion. There's the passage on
loving-kindness, almost a poem, that asks you to care for every living thing the way a
mother cares for her only child. There's the full step-by-step guide to mindfulness, the
practical core of the whole tradition. And there's the talk where he tells a town full of
skeptics not to believe a thing because it's repeated, inherited, or taught with
authority — to test it against their own lives instead. Even if the teacher is him.

This is a plain reading, not a scholarly translation: written for a first-time reader,
without giving up the substance. The repetitions the originals are built on are kept.

The cover credit reads "Translated by Claude Opus, edited by Alex Miller," because that's
what happened. The machine wrote a first draft. The book came out of hundreds of hours of
arguing with it, line by line, against the Pali and the standard translations — every
call made by a human, with a simple test: would this land on my kids, or my parents?

Read them slowly. Read them more than once. The work from here is yours.

## The six, as selling beats

1. **The Buddha's First Talk** — the middle way, and the four noble truths taught for the
   first time.
2. **The Buddha's Second Talk** — why the body and mind you call "you" aren't you.
3. **The Fire Sermon** — everything is burning with wanting, anger, and confusion. Let go.
4. **On Loving-Kindness** — goodwill for every living thing, with no one left out.
5. **The Foundations of Mindfulness** — a guided meditation from the Buddha himself.
6. **How to Decide What to Believe** — don't take anyone's word for it. Not even his.
