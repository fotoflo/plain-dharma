Plain Dharma. The Buddha's foundational teachings, in modern English. By Alex Miller.

These are six of the Buddha's foundational teachings — the first talk, not-self, the fire sermon, loving-kindness, the foundations of mindfulness (a guided meditation by the Buddha himself), and how to decide what to believe — rendered fresh for a first-time listener.

This is not a scholarly translation, but a plain reading — translated from the original Pali with the help of AI, then worked over by hand, word by word. The full story of how it was made is at the end.

It is released into the public domain under Creative Commons Zero — free to copy, share, and read aloud, with no permission needed, in keeping with the tradition of freely given dharma.
