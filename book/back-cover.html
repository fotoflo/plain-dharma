What the Buddha actually taught — before the schools, the systems, and the centuries of commentary.

The first three talks he gave. The instructions on loving-kindness. The foundations of mindfulness. And his answer to the question of how to decide what to believe.

Six teachings, rendered in plain modern English — not a scholarly translation, but a plain reading, meant to make the foundational suttas accessible to a first-time reader without sacrificing the substance.
