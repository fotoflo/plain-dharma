You've been listening to Plain Dharma — six of the Buddha's foundational teachings, in plain modern English.

A word about how this was made — there's an irony in it worth naming. These teachings are about what it is to be human, and yet this edition leaned on machines. The translation was first drafted from the original Pali by Claude, an A.I. language model. The illustrations were made by Google's Gemini. And this recording was first voiced by ElevenLabs — every word then argued over by hand against the twenty-six-hundred-year-old Pali, this narration edited by hand, and the artwork recolored and reworked.

Getting these teachings out to fellow humans was the first goal. Making the work more human is the next — and for that, we'd welcome your help: handmade artwork, a human reading, a fresh translation, or source materials of any kind. You'll find how to contribute at plaindharma dot com slash contribute, where this book is free in every format, alongside the paperback and the ebook.

It is released into the public domain — free to copy, share, and read aloud. May it be of benefit.
